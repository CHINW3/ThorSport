How it works

Two screens, two jobs.

Your stream plays on top. ThorSport runs underneath, and takes over both screens when you want it to.

Top: your stream Bottom: ThorSport Stick lights

  1. Leagues

    50+ leagues, sorted into folders.

    NHL, AHL, PWHL, NBA, WNBA, NFL, MLB and F1 sit at the top of the menu. Club soccer, international games, college sports, cricket and esports each get a folder. Hide anything you don't follow.

  2. My Teams

    Your teams come first.

    Long-press any team in any league to add it. MY TEAMS shows all their games on one screen, and only their goals set off the alerts.

  3. Goal celebrations

    A goal takes over the screen.

    Your team's colors, confetti, the scorer's name and a horn. Every NHL team has its own horn, and a synthesized sound pack covers touchdowns, home runs and wins.

  4. Stick lights

    The sticks wear your colors.

    The Thor's stick LEDs show your team's two main colors, one on each stick. They swap on a goal, turn gold on a win and pulse slowly in a tight finish.

  5. Two-screen mode

    The whole game on both screens.

    Press R3 and the top screen shows a giant Board: the NFL field, the baseball diamond or the NHL shot map. The bottom shows the game's stats and Polymarket win odds, graphed from the opening whistle.

Controller

Every screen works from the buttons.

The first press shows the blue selection. Touch the screen and it hides again.

  • D-padMove card by card
  • A / BOpen the game, go back
  • X / YStar the left or right team
  • L1 / R1Switch tabs
  • L2 / R2Switch leagues
  • R stickPrevious or next day
  • R3Two-screen mode on or off

Install

Install it in three steps.

ThorSport is an APK on GitHub. Open this page on your handheld, or download it on a computer and copy the file over.

  1. 1

    Download

    Tap Download APK. GitHub serves ThorSport.apk from the latest release.

    Download APK (opens GitHub in a new tab)
  2. 2

    Allow installs from your browser

    Open the file. When Android asks, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back.

  3. 3

    Open ThorSport

    Tap Install, then Open. On a Thor, if it opens on the top screen, long-press the icon and choose Screen Launch → Bottom Screen.

Updates come through the app: ThorSport shows a banner when a new version is out, and Settings → App update installs it. Release notes are on GitHub.
