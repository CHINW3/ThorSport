Install ThorSport

  1. Step 1

    Download

    Tap Download APK. GitHub serves ThorSport.apk from the latest release.

    Download APK (opens GitHub in a new tab)

  2. Step 2

    Allow installs from your browser

    Open the file. When Android asks, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source for your browser, then go back.

  3. Step 3

    Open ThorSport

    Tap Install, then Open. On a Thor, if it opens on the top screen, long-press the icon and choose Screen Launch → Bottom Screen.

Questions

What is ThorSport?

An Android app for live sports. On a dual-screen handheld it runs on the bottom screen with scores, standings, the Board and goal alerts, so the top screen stays free for the game you're watching.

Which devices does it work on?

It's built for the AYN Thor (bottom screen 1240×1080) and the Retroid Pocket Duo (1280×960), and draws to fit those screens exactly. It also works on a single-screen Android device.

Which leagues and sports are covered?

NHL, AHL, PWHL, NBA, WNBA, NFL, MLB, F1, the Premier League, MLS, the Champions League, La Liga, the Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1 and the EFL, college football, basketball and hockey, international soccer and hockey, cricket (Test, One-day, T20, women's and domestic) and esports (League of Legends, CS2, Valorant, Dota 2 and Rainbow Six Siege).

Where do the scores come from?

ESPN's public feeds for scores, standings and odds, the NHL's and MLB's own live feeds for faster goals and runs, HockeyTech for the PWHL, Jolpica for F1, lolesports.com for League of Legends and Polymarket for win chances.
